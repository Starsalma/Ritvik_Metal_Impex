Product Guide & Technical Article

Industrial Flanges: Types, Grades & Selection Guide

Flanges are critical pipe connection components that allow for easy assembly, disassembly, and maintenance of piping systems. They are used wherever pipes, valves, pumps, and equipment need to be securely connected.

01

Types of Flanges

Weld Neck flanges offer the highest integrity for critical service. Slip-On flanges are economical and easy to align. Blind flanges seal pipe ends. Socket Weld flanges suit small bore high-pressure lines. Lap Joint flanges allow rotation for bolt alignment. Spectacle blinds isolate sections for maintenance. Ring Joint flanges are used in high-pressure oil & gas service.

02

Pressure Ratings

Flanges are rated from Class 150# (low pressure) to Class 2500# (extreme pressure). The class determines the wall thickness, bolt circle, and number of bolts required for the connection.

03

Material Grades

Carbon Steel (ASTM A105), Stainless Steel (ASTM A182 F304/F304L/F316/F316L/F321/F347), Alloy Steel (ASTM A182 F5/F9/F11/F22/F91), Duplex Steel (UNS S31803/S32750), and Nickel Alloys.

04

Standards

Manufactured to ASME B16.5 (1/2" to 24" NB) and ASME B16.47 (26" to 60" NB) dimensional standards, with face finishes including raised face (RF), flat face (FF), and ring type joint (RTJ).
