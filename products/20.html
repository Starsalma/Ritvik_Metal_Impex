Product Guide & Technical Article

Alloy Bars: High Performance Grades for Demanding Applications

Alloy bars are engineered to perform where standard stainless steel falls short — in extreme temperatures, highly corrosive environments, or applications requiring superior strength and fatigue resistance.

01

Alloy Steel Grades

F5, F9 — high temperature creep resistance for power generation. F11/F22 — Cr-Mo alloys for pressure vessels and boilers. F91 — advanced Cr-Mo-V for supercritical power plants up to 650°C.

02

Nickel Alloy Grades

Monel 400 — seawater and acid resistance. Inconel 600/625 — high-temperature oxidation resistance. Inconel 718 — aerospace superalloy. Hastelloy C276/C22 — severe chemical environments.

03

Titanium Bars

Grade 2 — commercially pure, chemical processing. Grade 5 (Ti-6Al-4V) — high strength aerospace and medical grade with exceptional strength-to-weight ratio.

04

Testing & Certification

Full chemical analysis, mechanical testing, hardness, impact testing. PMI by XRF on request. Full material traceability for aerospace, defense, and nuclear sectors.
