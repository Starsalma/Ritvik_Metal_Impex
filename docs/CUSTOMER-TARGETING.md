# Who actually buys this, and where to find them

Derived from the 23 products in the catalogue, not from generic B2B advice.
Companion to `LEAD-GENERATION.md`, which covers converting a lead once it
arrives. This one is about where the leads come from in the first place.

---

## The catalogue is two businesses

This is the single most important thing in this document.

**Business A — process piping (16 products).** Pipes, tubes, buttweld and
forged fittings, flanges, bends, plates, bars, channels, valves, fasteners, in
304, 316, Duplex 2205, Inconel, Monel, Titanium Grade 2 and 904L. Buyers are
refineries, chemical plants, EPC contractors and fabricators.

**Business B — copper and brass (7 products).** Copper tubes, strips, profiles
and wire, super enamelled copper wire, brass tubes and rods. Buyers are
electrical manufacturers, transformer and motor makers, HVAC contractors and
turned-parts machine shops. **These people do not search for "stainless steel
supplier" and will never find you through the piping content.**

The website currently speaks almost entirely to Business A. Business B is seven
products — nearly a third of the catalogue — with no grade pages, no guides
aimed at it, and one thin article. If copper and brass are a meaningful share
of revenue, that is the largest untapped opportunity on the site.

## Segment 1 — Process piping and plant

**Products:** pipes, tubes, buttweld and forged fittings, flanges, bends,
plates, bars, channels and angles, valves.

**Who buys:**
- Plant maintenance and MRO buyers at refineries, chemical and fertiliser
  plants, power stations and pharma sites
- EPC and project procurement — L&T, Toyo, Tecnimont, Thermax, EIL and their
  subcontractors
- Fabricators and job shops working to a client drawing
- OEMs building process equipment — heat exchangers, vessels, skids

**How to reach them, in order of return:**

1. **Vendor registration.** In Indian industrial procurement this beats
   marketing. Get registered as an approved vendor with the EPCs and PSUs you
   already supply, and on **GeM (Government e-Marketplace)** for public sector
   buyers — NTPC, BHEL, IOCL, ONGC and state utilities all buy through it.
   Registration is free, takes paperwork rather than money, and puts you in
   front of tenders you currently never see. Your GST registration and mill
   test certification are most of what is required.
2. **Long-tail search.** This is what the grade and grade-form pages were
   built for. "inconel 625 pipe supplier", "duplex 2205 flanges mumbai".
3. **IndiaMART and TradeIndia paid listings.** Immediate volume while organic
   builds. See `GROWTH-PLAYBOOK.md` for what they actually sell you.
4. **Shutdown and turnaround season.** Refineries and plants schedule
   shutdowns, and MRO buying spikes before them. Ask existing customers when
   theirs are, and contact them six to eight weeks ahead.

**What wins:** stock availability, a firm price within hours, a mill test
certificate, and IBR or third-party inspection when the project requires it.

## Segment 2 — Hygienic and sanitary

**Products:** dairy fittings, electro-polished fittings, ferrule fittings.

This is a specialist niche with specialist buyers, and it is worth separating
because the people who buy it never use the words "stainless steel stockist".

**Who buys:**
- Dairy and beverage plant builders and their fabricators
- Pharmaceutical equipment OEMs — reactors, blenders, clean-in-place skids
- Food processing equipment manufacturers
- Instrumentation and analyser panel builders, for ferrule fittings
  specifically

**Where they are in India:** the dairy and food equipment cluster around
Gujarat and Maharashtra; pharmaceutical manufacturing around Hyderabad,
Ahmedabad and Baddi.

**How to reach them:**
- Direct approach to equipment OEMs rather than end plants — the OEM buys
  repeatedly, the plant buys once
- Trade exhibitions are unusually effective here: India Pharma, Dairy Industry
  Expo, ProPak
- Content on surface finish, Ra values, electropolishing and 3-A / ASME BPE
  requirements — a page on this would serve a search nothing on the site
  currently answers

**What wins:** surface finish specification, documentation, consistency.

## Segment 3 — Fasteners

**Products:** stud bolts, nuts, washers, threaded rod, in stainless, alloy,
carbon steel and Titanium Grade 2.

Fasteners are usually bought alongside flanges, so most of this demand arrives
with Segment 1. Two exceptions worth pursuing separately:

- **Titanium and exotic fasteners** are a specialist search with little
  competition and high margin. "titanium grade 2 bolts supplier" has genuine
  intent behind it and few credible Indian pages answering it.
- **Shutdown fastener packages** — a refinery turnaround consumes large
  quantities of stud bolts to a fixed specification, ordered in advance.

## Segment 4 — Copper and brass (the neglected half)

**Products:** copper tubes, strips, profiles, wires, super enamelled copper
wire, brass tubes and rods.

Each of these has a different buyer, and none of them is a piping buyer.

| Product | Who actually buys it |
|---|---|
| Super enamelled copper wire | Transformer manufacturers, motor and alternator winders, coil and choke makers |
| Copper tubes | HVAC and refrigeration contractors, heat exchanger builders, plumbing |
| Copper strips and profiles | Switchgear and busbar manufacturers, earthing contractors |
| Copper wires | Electrical contractors, cable and harness makers |
| Brass rods | CNC turned-component manufacturers — sanitary fittings, valves, connectors |
| Brass tubes | Heat exchanger and instrument manufacturers, decorative fabrication |

**Where they cluster in India — and this matters:**

- **Jamnagar** is India's brass parts capital. Thousands of turned-component
  workshops consuming brass rod continuously. It is already in the site's
  `areasServed` list, and it is a directly addressable market for one product.
- **Vadodara, Ahmedabad and Delhi NCR** for transformer and motor
  manufacturing, which consumes super enamelled copper wire.
- **Pune, Chennai and Coimbatore** for motors, pumps and switchgear.

**How to reach them:**
- These are repeat, high-frequency buyers. A transformer maker buys enamelled
  wire every month, not once a project. Winning one is worth far more than a
  single piping enquiry, so direct sales effort is justified.
- Search demand exists and is uncontested by you: "super enamelled copper wire
  supplier", "brass rod supplier jamnagar", "copper busbar strip supplier".
  There is currently no page on the site targeting any of it.
- Industry associations: IEEMA for electrical equipment, and the regional
  brass parts associations in Jamnagar.

**Recommendation:** if copper and brass matter commercially, they need the same
treatment stainless just received — grade or alloy pages, guides aimed at
electrical and HVAC buyers, and their own enquiry path. Say the word and it is
a week of work rather than a rebuild.

## Export markets

The site already claims the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman, the UK, the USA,
Singapore and Malaysia.

**What actually generates export enquiries:**
- Search, in English, using ASTM rather than IS standards — which the grade
  pages now do
- Export documentation stated plainly: packing, marking, LC terms, third-party
  inspection
- Response time across time zones. A Gulf buyer enquiring at 09:00 their time
  expects an answer that morning
- Alibaba and ExportersIndia for volume, though quality is mixed

**Most realistic first market:** the Gulf. Shared time zone, heavy process
industry, large Indian buying community, established India–UAE trade routes.

## The order to do this in

1. **Vendor registrations, starting with GeM.** Free, unglamorous, and it
   opens tenders you cannot currently see.
2. **Google Business Profile and Search Console.** Still not done, still the
   largest free wins. See `SEARCH-CONSOLE-AND-GBP.md`.
3. **Ask your existing customers what else they buy.** The cheapest lead in
   this industry. A customer buying 316 pipe may buy fasteners and gaskets
   from someone else purely because they never knew you stocked them.
4. **Decide about copper and brass.** If it is real revenue, give it pages. If
   it is not, consider whether seven products deserve the catalogue space.
5. **Shutdown calendars.** Ask every process-plant customer when their next
   turnaround is and diarise a call eight weeks before.
6. **Then** paid search on specification keywords.
